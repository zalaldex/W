Add a Statistics button to your existing settings_menu.go.

Your current settings_menu.go builds a keyboard with Word / Sentence /
Paragraph / Full buttons plus (presumably) a Back button. Add one more row:

    var statsBtn = menu.Text("📊 Statistics")

and include statsBtn in the ReplyKeyboard rows returned by settingsMenu(),
e.g.:

    menu.Reply(
        menu.Row(wordBtn, sentenceBtn),
        menu.Row(paragraphBtn, fullBtn),
        menu.Row(statsBtn),
        menu.Row(backBtn),
    )

The exact line numbers depend on your file, since it wasn't provided — but
the two things register_stats_handlers.go needs from you are:
  1. A btn.Text("📊 Statistics") (or matching label) added to your settings
     reply keyboard.
  2. The same text string used in a bot.Handle(tele.Text, ...) or
     bot.Handle(&statsBtn, ...) match in register_stats_handlers.go — see
     that file, which currently matches on the literal text "📊 Statistics".

If your label differs, update the string in register_stats_handlers.go to
match exactly.
